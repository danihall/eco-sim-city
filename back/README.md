## raison d'être
This project aims to implicitly raise questions about contemporary issues related to the life of a city's citizen.

The goal is to try to subvert the game mechanics of classic city-builders, who reward an extractivist, expansionist and capitalistically unhinged/unbridled logic. This gives rise to opportunities to hint at players the effects of applying the underlying ideology of games like SimCity or Civilization: a socially unjust world that destroys the environnment.

This is reflected in how the data's structure represents a living city; the core idea is that, from a city's data, naturally emerges the issues I want to pinpoint.

While admittely politically biased, I try to present to players the dilemnas of building and managing a city whithin its economic, social and environmental constraints.

Technical documentation can be found in `./internal`.
