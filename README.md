This project aims to implicitly raise questions about contemporary issues related to the life of a city's citizen.

The goal is to try to subvert the game mechanics of classic city-builders, and make players ponder about the consequences of their actions.

Managing a city involves making choices, and I try to drive players'decisions toward a sustainable city, in a pedagogic effort.
But of course, one can create a city with different goals in mind, which will impact citizen lifes in different ways.

This is reflected in how the data's structure represents a living city; the core idea is that, from a city's data, naturally emerges the issues I want to pinpoint.
While admittely politically biased, I try to present to players the dilemnas of building and managing a city whithin realistic economic, social and environmental constraints.
